HotkeyPad Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

HotkeyPad does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server. The app connects directly and privately to a HotkeyPad host running on your own Mac, over Bluetooth or your local Wi-Fi network. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere else.

What HotkeyPad does

HotkeyPad lets a phone act as a remote control for a Mac. The HotkeyPad host app runs on your Mac; the HotkeyPad client app runs on your phone. When you pair them, the phone talks directly to that one Mac — there is no cloud service, no account, and no intermediary server in between.

Bluetooth

The client app uses Bluetooth Low Energy only to discover a nearby HotkeyPad host and to exchange button presses with it once paired. This connection stays entirely between your phone and your Mac.

Local network / Wi-Fi

The client app uses your local Wi-Fi network only to find a HotkeyPad host on the same network and to communicate with it directly, the same way a printer or a smart-home device on your network works. The first time you connect to a new host, a one-time PIN shown on the host confirms it's really your Mac before the phone is trusted — this pairing decision is stored only on the host and the phone involved, never anywhere else.

Camera

The client app can use the camera to scan a QR code shown on the host, as a faster alternative to typing in its address by hand. The camera feed is processed on the phone only to read that QR code; it is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

What HotkeyPad does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No data is uploaded to any server operated by us or anyone else — the app has no server to upload to.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

HotkeyPad is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — see above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with an updated date at the top.
