HotkeyPad turns your phone into a custom macro deck for your Mac — a grid of buttons you design yourself, each one launching an app, running a keyboard shortcut, controlling media playback, or firing off a whole sequence of steps at once.

Pair it with the free HotkeyPad Host app on your Mac (over Bluetooth or WiFi, whichever the two find first) and start building:

• App launcher buttons — jump straight to any app on your Mac
• Keyboard shortcuts and key combinations
• Media controls — play/pause, skip, volume
• Shell commands, for anything else your Mac can run
• Combo buttons that chain several steps together with your own timing
• Multiple pages, so your most-used shortcuts are never more than a swipe away
• Custom icons and a background image, so your deck looks like yours

No account, no cloud, no subscription — HotkeyPad talks directly to your own Mac on your own network.

Requires the HotkeyPad Host app running on a Mac.
